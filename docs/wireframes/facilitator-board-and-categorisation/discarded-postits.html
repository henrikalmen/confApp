What happens when you put one back

  • A restored post-it returns to Uncategorised — never to the category it was in. The discard decision is undone; the sorting decision is not assumed.
  • You can restore one at any time until this conference is archived. This page keeps them until then; nothing here expires and there is no countdown.
  • While they are here they are on no other surface. They are not on the board, not on the projected screen, not on anyone's phone, and not on their own author's — with no marker and no notification anywhere.

Interaction notes (annotation, not part of the surface)

  • This is a page, not an undo. It has an address, it is linked from the sorting surface's toolbar whether or not anything was just discarded, and it can be left and returned to. Nothing on it disappears on a timer.
  • Every entry carries its trace — who discarded it and when — beside the post-it's own author. That trace is the whole difference between a Discard and an author deleting their own post-it, which leaves nothing at all.
  • Restore is per post-it and states its destination in the control itself, so the rule that a restore goes to Uncategorised is read before it is exercised, not discovered afterwards.
  • Permanent removal is offered here, to an admin only. Amended 2026-08-31 (see design-decisions.md → “The discarded Post-its surface” → the 2026-08-31 amendment). This wireframe was drawn on 2026-08-30, when the settled decision was that no permanent-removal control appears here, and it is left as drawn. The shipped surface renders a per-post-it permanent-removal control beside – never instead of – the restore control, gated on the server-supplied canRemovePermanently flag: a facilitator without conference-wide admin is answered false and sees exactly the page drawn here. The reason for the change is that discarded post-its had left the board, so the only route to removing one was to restore it first, putting the content back in front of the room in order to take it away.
