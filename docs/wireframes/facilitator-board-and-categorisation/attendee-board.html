Interaction notes (annotation, not part of the surface)

  • No post-it here carries a place, move or discard control — not somebody else's, and not your own. Sorting happens on one surface only, and it is not this one.
  • Your own post-it still carries Correct and Remove while the round is open, wherever the facilitator has put it. That is the author-deletion path the board already ships — it leaves no trace and it is yours alone to do — and it is a different act from a discard, which only a facilitator makes and which can be undone. Read-only here means read-only about placement, not about your own words.
  • A pending post-it sits inside Uncategorised, marked and named, because that is where it lands when it sends. It is not in a separate list below the board: that would read as a fourth place a post-it can be, and there are only three — Uncategorised, a category, or discarded.
  • The count beside Uncategorised is the server's number. A pending item is excluded from it, so no count on this surface is derived on the device and no two devices can disagree about how many post-its a region holds.
  • A discarded post-it is simply gone from here — including your own. No marker, no "set aside" note, no notification. Someone who wants the idea back writes a new post-it while the round is open.
  • Categories appear in the facilitator's order, the same order the projected screen shows, so someone at the back comparing the two sees one board.
