Interaction notes (annotation, not part of the surface)

  • Placement needs no drag and no pointer. Every post-it carries a labelled destination control listing Uncategorised and every category by name, plus a Move button. Placing from Uncategorised, moving between categories and moving back to Uncategorised are the same three keystrokes in all three directions. The destination is named in the label and in the option text, never by where a region sits on screen.
  • No drag affordance appears at any width, 1280px included. Stories S02 and S03 both decline to build drag, so drawing a handle or a drop hint would put an unimplementable control into an artifact five stories treat as authoritative.
  • Reorder is explicit. Move up / Move down name the resulting position in the button itself, and the region states its own position in words. The control at the end of the order is disabled rather than absent, so the set of controls does not change shape as a category moves.
  • Discard is per post-it and reversible. It sits on every post-it, in Uncategorised and in every category alike. It is never worded as deletion or as permanent removal — permanent removal is admin-only and is not on this surface at all.
  • Nothing here is queued. Sorting is online-only: a failed move states the failure and the post-it stays visibly where it was. No control offers to retry later.
