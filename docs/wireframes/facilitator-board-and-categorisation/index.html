Wireframes — Facilitator board view and post-it categorisation

Story S01 · Four surfaces across two viewport classes. The facilitator's and the attendee's surfaces are validated at 375 / 768 / 1280 px; the projected board view is a fourth viewport class, validated at 1920×1080 and never signed off as the 1280 px capture with a larger font.

Grayscale and structural throughout. No colour, no external asset, no network request, and no drag affordance at any width.

  • Facilitator sorting surface

    375 / 768 / 1280 px

    The one control surface. Placement without drag or pointer, category management (create, rename, non-drag reorder, remove), a discard control on every post-it, and the permanent entry to the discarded post-its. Shows the occupied-category removal and rename states.

    Open facilitator-sorting.html

  • Discarded post-its

    375 / 768 / 1280 px

    The only place a discard is reversed. A page with an address, not a toast or a timed undo: who discarded each post-it and when, a restore per post-it that names Uncategorised as its destination, and the statement that the window runs to archival.

    Open discarded-postits.html

  • Attendee's live board

    375 / 768 / 1280 px

    The same board on a phone, read-only with respect to placement — no place, move or discard control on any post-it, the attendee's own included. Shows where a pending post-it sits: inside the Uncategorised region, excluded from its count.

    Open attendee-board.html

  • Projected board view — design ceiling

    Projection · 1920×1080

    20 categories plus Uncategorised and 200 post-its on one screen. Every category and count visible, nothing hidden behind an interaction, and no control of any kind. Post-it detail degrades per category through three tiers; names, counts and boundaries never do.

    Open projected-board-view.html

  • Projected board view — empty board

    Projection · 1920×1080

    Zero post-its. The categories render with counts of 0 and the grid sizes itself to them — a legitimate pre-round state on the big screen, not an error.

    Open projected-board-empty.html

    Projection · 1920×1080

    One neutral message for revoked, expired, still-draft, deleted-round and never-existed alike, saying nothing about which. No control, and deliberately not even a retry.
